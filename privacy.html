Privacy Policy

Website: scrapkatta.com
Effective Date: 31 October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Scrapkatta. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how Scrapkatta collects, uses, stores and protects information when you visit our website, request scrap pickup services or contact us.

By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Personal Information: Your name, mobile number and email address.
  • Pickup Information: Your pickup address, location and preferred pickup date and time.
  • Scrap Details: Type of scrap, estimated quantity, weight and photographs uploaded by you.
  • Review Information: The name, area, rating and review text you submit. Reviews are shown on our website only after we approve them.
  • Communication Information: WhatsApp messages, calls, enquiries and customer feedback.
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, device information and website usage data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process scrap pickup requests.
  • Contact customers to confirm pickup details.
  • Provide estimated scrap rates and quotations.
  • Arrange doorstep scrap collection.
  • Communicate regarding pickup schedules and services.
  • Improve our website and customer experience.
  • Prevent fraudulent activities and maintain service security.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share relevant information with:

  • Pickup personnel and collection partners to complete your booking.
  • Technology and hosting providers to operate our website.
  • Communication service providers to respond to customer enquiries.
  • Government authorities when required by applicable law.

We only share information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

5. Cookies and Website Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Improve website performance.
  • Understand visitor behaviour.
  • Remember certain website preferences.
  • Measure advertising and marketing performance.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, misuse, loss or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain customer information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, complete transactions, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete or securely dispose of it.

8. Your Privacy Rights

  • Request access to your personal information.

  • Request deletion of your personal information where applicable.
